RUBS utility billing

A workbook for splitting utility bills across units, with the formulas left in the file.

  • Open the workbook
  • Full notes

What it shows

Three synthetic properties (4, 8, and 12 units). Bills for water, sewer, trash, gas, and common-area electric. A mid-month move-in, a move-out and a later move-in, a vacant unit, a unit excluded from gas, a 10% discount, and trash that is partly owner-paid. Vacant days and discounts land on an Owner line. The cents reconcile.

What it is not

The data is synthetic. RUBS rules vary by US state and local law. This is a calculation tool, not legal advice, and not a live billing system. Credits (negative amounts) are flagged and not allocated. Overlapping occupancy is flagged, not repaired. Recalculation was checked in LibreOffice, not by claiming a specific Excel build.
