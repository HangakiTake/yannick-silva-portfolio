Travel agency finance workbook

An Excel finance system for a fictional B2B travel agency: bookings (individual and group), profit per booking, invoices, supplier bills, full and partial payments, expenses, EUR/BAM/USD, a monthly dashboard and reports. Excel Tables with SUMIFS and XLOOKUP, no macros. All names and figures are fictional.

How it was tested

An automated test suite recalculates the workbook in LibreOffice and an independent Python recheck rebuilds every figure from the typed inputs only (706/706 figures match).

  • 10 tests pass: profit per booking (40/40), partial payments (10/10 scripted cases), base-currency switch (3/3), dashboard tie-out (19/19 checks), reports (4/4), aging buckets (12/12), validation (8/8 bad inputs flagged), extensibility (add 5 bookings, everything updates), blank copy (0 errors).
  • 1 test fails: T10 scale. At 2,000 bookings / 5,000 payments a full recalculation took 11.9 s in LibreOffice against a 3 s target. Excel was not available to time it.
  • 2 tests need a person in Excel: T08 sheet protection and T12 handover (someone else using only the guide).

Requirements: 17 of 20 met, 2 partial (R10, R13), 1 not met (R19, the scale target).

Honest caveats: tested in LibreOffice, not desktop Excel. The scale target is missed in LibreOffice. Protection and handover still need a manual check in Excel.
